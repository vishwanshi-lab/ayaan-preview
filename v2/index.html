Questions

Before you book

How does valet parking work?

Your guest arrives and is greeted by a uniformed attendant, who takes the vehicle and provides a ticket. When they are ready to leave, the ticket is presented and the car is returned promptly.

Is valet parking safe?

Yes. We employ trained, insured professionals who follow strict security protocols to ensure your vehicle is handled with care and parked in a secure area.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the venue, the hours and the number of guests. Contact us and we will quote against your specific requirements.

Can you accommodate special requests?

Yes. Concierge options include luggage assistance, specific drop-off arrangements and more. Let us know in advance and we will plan around it.

Do you cover private residences?

Yes. We provide valet for private homes and residential communities, for gatherings and for everyday guest parking.

Can you handle large events?

Yes. We are experienced with large-scale events and high-traffic venues, and staff the team to the size of the crowd.
